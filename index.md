Welcome! I’m a PhD student specializing in Public Law in the Department of Government and a Graduate Research Fellow at the Center for Law and Democracy at the University of Texas at Austin. My research interests focus on judicial politics, judicial behavior, and public opinion toward legal elites.

I received a B.A. in Political Science from National Cheng Kung University in Taiwan and an M.Sc. in Political Science from Universiteit Leiden in the Netherlands.
